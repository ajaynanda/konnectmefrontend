Section 01

Core Architectural Principles

Unlike legacy social platforms whose revenue depends on surveillance capitalism, KonnectMe is financed strictly through voluntary creator memberships, verified badge licensing, and enterprise studio infrastructure.

This alignment ensures we have zero financial incentive to harvest your emotional reactions, track your keystrokes, sell your contact details to predatory third parties, or design rage-inducing feeds to artificially boost screen time.

Zero Ad Trackers

Zero pixel scripts, zero cross-site cookies, and no telemetry shared with ad exchanges.

True Chronology

Your feeds reflect genuine network timestamps. Posts are never hidden behind pay-to-boost algorithms.

Section 03

Zero Ad-Monetization Model

We make a permanent pledge: We will never monetize our users by selling access to their attention.

Specifically, KonnectMe does NOT:

  • Sell, lease, or license user profiles, connection graphs, or contact directories to marketing brokers.
  • Build behavioral profiles based on your reading speeds, photo zoom interactions, or audio room attendance.
  • Inject sponsored posts, deceptive affiliate placements, or dark-pattern promotional notifications into your timeline.

When you see content on KonnectMe, it appears strictly because an individual creator or organization you follow published it, or because you proactively searched for it.

Section 04

Storage & Client-Side Encryption

Security at KonnectMe is rooted in defense-in-depth engineering. We employ modern cryptographic standards across every layer of transmission and storage:

End-to-End Keying

Direct private messages utilize Double Ratchet cryptographic protocols so only you and the recipient hold decryption keys.

Encrypted at Rest

All database records, portfolio snapshots, and media buckets are encrypted with AES-256 standards.

Data is hosted in sovereign, ISO 27001-certified and SOC 2 Type II compliant cloud clusters located in the European Union and North America.
